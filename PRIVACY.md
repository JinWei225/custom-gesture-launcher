# Privacy Policy — Gessearch Launcher

_Effective 27 October 2026_

Gessearch Launcher is a home-screen app for Android, published by JinWei225. This policy explains
what information the app uses and what happens to it.

**In short: the app collects nothing, sends nothing anywhere, and has no internet access.**
Everything it uses stays on your device.

## No internet access

The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot connect to any server. It
contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind. When you search the
web, open a link or ask an AI app a question, the app hands the request to your browser or that
app, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

## Information the app uses, all on your device

| What | Why | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Calendar events** (only if you grant calendar permission) | To show today's events beside the clock | Read on your device when the home screen is shown. Never stored or copied by the app. |
| **List of installed apps** | To show them in the app drawer and search, and to launch them | Kept on your device as a cache so the drawer opens quickly |
| **Your gestures, app aliases, notes, widget layout and settings** | So the launcher works the way you set it up | Saved in the app's private storage on your device |
| **A font file you choose** (optional) | To use it as the app's font | A private copy is kept on your device |
| **Your voice** (only if you grant microphone permission, and only while you hold the dock or use the mic button) | To turn what you say into a search | See below |
| **Voice words you add** (optional) | So voice search recognises names it would otherwise mishear | Saved in the app's private storage, and given to the speech service as described below |

Searches, calculations and time-zone lookups are worked out on your device and are not stored.

## Voice search

When you hold the strip beside the search button or use the mic in quick search, the app asks your
phone's speech recognition service (on most phones, Google's) to listen and turn what you say into
text. The audio goes to that service, not to this app: the app receives only the text, uses it as
a search, and does not record or store either. To help the service recognise app names it would
not otherwise know, the app also gives it the names of your installed apps, the aliases you have
set and the voice words you have added, for that request only. The service may process the audio on your device
or send it to its provider's servers, under that provider's privacy policy. The app's own lack of
internet access is unchanged.

## Accessibility service

The app includes an accessibility service. It is off unless you turn it on yourself in Android's
Accessibility settings, and the app asks for your agreement before sending you there. It is used
for one feature only: **Alt + Space on a physical keyboard opens quick search from any app.** The
service receives the keys you press on a physical keyboard, acts only on Alt + Space, and passes
every other key straight on. Key presses are not recorded, stored or shared.

The service cannot read what is on your screen; it is configured without that capability. You can
turn it off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.

## Backups

- **Export backup** in the app's settings writes your gestures, aliases, voice words and settings to a file in a
  location you pick. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.
- If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include this app's data in the
  backup it stores in your Google account. That backup is made and controlled by Android and
  Google under your account settings, not by this app.

## Sharing

The app shares no information with anyone. There are no third-party services in it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. You can also clear it at any time in Android's
Settings → Apps → Gessearch Launcher → Storage → Clear data.
